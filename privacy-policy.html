POLICY.

How Rise City handles your information, including text messaging consent.

01Our commitment

Rise City respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, protect, and disclose information when you visit risecity.now, contact our ministry, use our connection card, schedule a meeting, give, or choose to receive text messages.

02Information we collect

We may receive information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, optional phone number, communication preferences, interests, messages, prayer requests, appointment details, and information needed to respond to a gift or other inquiry. If you opt in to SMS, we may retain your number, consent choice, date and source of consent, message history, and opt-out status. Website hosting and service providers may also process technical information such as IP address, browser information, device information, and access logs. Please avoid submitting sensitive medical, financial, or other confidential information through our general connection form.

03How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries and prayer requests, help people connect with Rise City, coordinate meetings and ministry activities, communicate event and Bible study information, administer gifts, maintain our website and records, protect against misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. We send SMS communications only to people who have given the appropriate consent, subject to applicable law. Providing a phone number by itself is not consent to recurring text messages.

04Mobile information and SMS privacy

We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties for their independent use. Service providers that support our communications may process information only as needed to provide those services on our behalf, such as delivering messages or recording consent. We do not make mobile numbers or opt-in lists available to unrelated organizations for their own marketing. You can withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP to a Rise City text. For assistance, reply HELP or email us.

05Service providers and other disclosures

Our website and ministry communications may use service providers for hosting (GitHub Pages), connection-form delivery (FormSubmit), email and appointment scheduling (Google), messaging (Gloo), and giving through Zelle and participating financial institutions. These providers may process information needed to operate their services, subject to their own privacy practices and applicable agreements. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or security. The SMS marketing and consent restrictions above apply to our handling of mobile information. We do not collect payment card or bank login details through this website; giving is completed using your own financial provider.

06Cookies and external websites

Our website may use, or link to services that use, cookies and similar technologies for functionality, security, and service delivery. Social media, appointment booking, payment services, and other external sites have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies before providing personal information.

07Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the information entrusted to us. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including applicable recordkeeping, consent documentation, opt-out records, and legal obligations.

08Your choices and privacy requests

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete information you have provided, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping needs. To stop SMS messages, reply STOP. To stop other communications or update your preferences, contact us by email. We may retain limited opt-out information to honor your request.

09Children’s privacy

Our general online connection form is intended for adults or for use with the involvement of a parent or guardian. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission so we can address the situation.

10Updates and contact

We may update this policy as our ministry or services change. The updated policy will be posted on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact Rise City at risecitynow@gmail.com. See our Terms of Use for SMS program details.
