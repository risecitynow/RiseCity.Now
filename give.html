02 / GIVING MADE SIMPLE

HOW TO GIVE.

It only takes a few steps to give through Zelle.

01

Open your banking app.

Sign in to a participating bank or credit union and open the Zelle feature under payments or transfers.

02

Enter our giving email.

Add risecitynow@gmail.com as the recipient. Carefully check the recipient information before proceeding.

03

Choose your gift.

Enter the amount you wish to give and review the details in your banking app.

04

Confirm and send.

Send your gift from the Zelle screen in your banking app. Keep its confirmation for your records.

Not seeing Zelle? Ask your bank or credit union whether it offers Zelle transfers.

04 / GOOD TO KNOW

FREQUENTLY
ASKED QUESTIONS.

Can I give directly on this website?+

No. This page provides the giving email and instructions. You complete the transfer securely through Zelle in your bank or credit union app.

What email should I use?+

Enter risecitynow@gmail.com as the Zelle recipient email. Confirm that the recipient information displayed by your bank matches your intended recipient before sending.

Can I cancel or reverse a Zelle gift?+

Zelle transfers to enrolled recipients generally cannot be reversed. Verify all details before sending. For transfer questions, contact your bank or credit union.

Can I request information about a gift?+

For questions about a gift sent to Rise City, please email risecitynow@gmail.com and include the transfer date and amount. Do not email account numbers or sensitive banking information.

Can I give a recurring amount?+

Scheduling options depend on your bank or credit union. Check its Zelle settings to see whether recurring transfers are supported.
