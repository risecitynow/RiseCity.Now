01About these terms

These Terms of Use apply to the Rise City website at risecity.now and the ministry communications and services described here. By using this website, you agree to these terms. Text messaging participation is voluntary and requires separate consent; using the website alone does not enroll you in SMS.

02Rise City SMS program

When you opt in to receive text messages from Rise City, you may receive ministry communications such as responses to your requests, connection follow-ups, event announcements and reminders, Bible study and worship updates, appointment coordination, prayer-related communications, and other ministry information. Messages may be recurring. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier’s plan determines any charges.

03Consent to receive messages

You may opt in through a Rise City form or another consent method that clearly describes the texting program. Consent is voluntary and is not a condition of giving, attending, purchasing anything, or using our website. Please provide a mobile number you are authorized to use. Notify us if your number changes or is reassigned. We do not treat an optional phone number, appointment booking, or donation alone as consent to recurring SMS.

04How to stop messages or get help

Reply STOP to a Rise City text to opt out of SMS communications. You may receive a final message confirming your opt-out. To receive texts again, you must opt in again. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact risecitynow@gmail.com. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

05Privacy

Our Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we handle it, including mobile numbers and text messaging opt-in information. We will not sell or share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent information for third-party or affiliate marketing or promotional purposes.

06Website and ministry information

We provide information about our ministry, beliefs, gatherings, and opportunities to connect. Event schedules and availability may change. Please use the website lawfully and respectfully, and do not interfere with its operation or submit misleading, harmful, or unsolicited material. Information on this site is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or emergency assistance.

07External services and giving

Links to third-party services, including appointment scheduling, social media, and giving, are provided for convenience. Those services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. When giving through Zelle, verify the recipient and payment details in your banking app before sending.

08Availability and changes

We aim to provide accurate, accessible information, but do not guarantee uninterrupted website access or delivery of communications. We may update these terms as our services change. Updates will be posted on this page; we will obtain renewed SMS consent when required.

09Contact us

For website, ministry, or messaging questions, contact Rise City at risecitynow@gmail.com. For SMS assistance, reply HELP; to opt out, reply STOP.
