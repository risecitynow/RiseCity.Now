RISE CITY — PRIVACY POLICY & TERMS OF USE
For Gloo toll-free verification

1. Before publishing: review both policies for accuracy. Confirm that the legal entity name is Rise City (or adjust it to match your Gloo/EIN paperwork), the contact email is monitored, and the described processing, retention and security practices are accurate. In particular, only publish the mobile-number/opt-in non-sharing commitment if you actually follow it. Do not share or sell SMS consent data for marketing. Verify the proposed opt-in disclosure matches your real texting program. Have a qualified attorney review the drafts. These files are not legal advice or a guarantee of carrier approval.

2. Upload these four website files to the ROOT of your existing GitHub repository:
   privacy-policy.html
   terms-of-use.html
   legal.css
   legal-links.js
   Do not replace existing files, content JSON, CNAME, CSS, or .pages.yml.

3. In GitHub, open the CURRENT page-links.js, click Edit, and append the contents of APPEND-TO-page-links.js.txt at the very end. Do not replace the existing script. Commit. This adds discreet, visible footer links across pages that load the shared script, and inserts the voluntary SMS opt-in and legal links on Connect. The legal pages have static links to each other and to the Connect page even without JavaScript. No existing menu or coffee buttons are changed. If any page does not load page-links.js, add <script defer src="legal-links.js"></script> to its head or add the legal links directly to its footer.

4. Verify these URLs in a private/incognito browser after GitHub Pages publishes:
   https://risecity.now/privacy-policy.html
   https://risecity.now/terms-of-use.html
   https://risecity.now/connect.html
   Check footer links from Home and Connect, and the form's unchecked SMS box. If checked, the form requires a phone number and includes the consent wording and choice in its normal FormSubmit email. No automatic Gloo enrollment is implemented; keep actual consent evidence, and only add contacts to Gloo using a supported, consent-compliant workflow. Honor STOP and HELP requests. If the form is not your actual SMS opt-in method, put the same disclosures and policy links at the actual opt-in point too. Do not enroll someone who merely enters their phone number. Verify that FormSubmit is active before accepting consent through this form.

5. Submit the two PUBLIC HTTPS policy URLs to Gloo. Use your actual legal organization name and details, and verify that your policies and opt-in evidence match the messaging program. Check both URLs without logging in. Gloo and its carrier partners determine approval.

Source: https://help.gloo.us/en/articles/16603739-submitting-a-privacy-policy-terms-of-use-for-toll-free-verification
